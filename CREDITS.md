Before canon was cut down to protected sections, it packaged community work as a project setup. Those components were removed from the main line and are preserved, with their full credits, on the [`archive/canon-full`](https://github.com/Orthogon-AI-Labs/canon/tree/archive/canon-full) branch ([CREDITS.md there](https://github.com/Orthogon-AI-Labs/canon/blob/archive/canon-full/CREDITS.md)).
